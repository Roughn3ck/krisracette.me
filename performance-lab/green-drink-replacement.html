GREEN DRINK REPLACEMENT

Greens powder out. Engineered soup in.

A documented self-experiment in quantified nutrition: hypothesis, data, verdict. One premium greens supplement replaced by a rotating breakfast soup — with the full label math and the trade-offs left in.

Kris Racette × Deschamps (nutrition systems) · 21 Sep 2026 · Brisbane

Hypothesis

A food-first breakfast — built from real vegetables, bone broth, tofu, and fermented sides — can replace a premium greens powder for about a third of the monthly cost, while delivering more protein, more fibre, and more actual vegetables per serve.

The test: run both systems' numbers side by side — product labels against receipts — then eat the replacement daily, track it, and report honestly. This page is the record.

The Replacement — Rotating Breakfast Soup

Two variants share the same vegetable chassis and rotate through the week. Both are batch-prepped, portioned, and reheated. Every macro below is verified against product labels and receipts.

Variant A — Miso Bone Broth & Tofu

  • - 500ml bone broth base
  • - Broccoli, cap mushroom, spinach & kale
  • - Brown onion, garlic, olive oil
  • - 150g tofu · bone broth concentrate
  • - White miso — stirred in OFF heat
  • - Kimchi / sauerkraut side (cold)

Role: carries the food-first probiotics

Variant B — Laksa Tofu

  • - Same vegetable chassis as Variant A
  • - Laksa paste bloomed in the batch
  • - 150g tofu · bone broth concentrate
  • - Dried wakame strip — iodine
  • - Kimchi / sauerkraut side (cold)

Role: covers the iodine the greens drink used to do

The oil dial matters: as-written in the recipe book (4 tbsp) this is a training-day breakfast at ~826 kcal. The daily version caps oil at 1–2 tbsp → ~456–578 kcal. Both versions ship in the recipe book.

Probiotic Method — Food-First

The greens drink's 8B CFU are encapsulated. The replacement gets its cultures from food, two ways:

  • 1. Miso stirred in OFF heat — boiling miso kills its live cultures. Stirred in at serving temperature, they stay alive.
  • 2. 15–30g kimchi or sauerkraut on the side, daily, kept cold.

Reasoning, per published research: the Stanford fermented-food trial (Cell, 2021) found a diet high in fermented foods increased microbiome diversity and steadied immune markers, while the fibre-only arm and capsule-based supplements showed weaker, more variable effects. Food-first was the read.

That study describes its trial participants — not this experiment. No claims are being made here about what this stack does for anyone else.

Micronutrient Insurance

The greens powder's 24 vitamins/minerals at 75–900% RDI don't get hand-waved away — they get replaced deliberately:

  • → Basic multivitamin, ~$5–8/month — covers B12, D3, zinc, selenium.
  • → Dried wakame in the laksa variant — iodine, the other job the greens drink was doing.

Verdict

Kept. The replacement holds on every axis the experiment set: cheaper, denser in protein/fibre/vegetables, and the two jobs the greens drink did best — probiotics and iodine — are covered deliberately, not by accident.

The rotation is the system: Variant A (miso) carries the cultures, Variant B (laksa + wakame) carries the iodine, and the oil dial switches the same chassis between a ~456–578 kcal daily breakfast and an ~826 kcal training-day feed.
